A one-line entry for make.fun's *The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger*: "i'm at 1%. i waited by the outlet all day; you were with the tablet. bigger battery, i get it. i thought we had a connection. don't text back—i can't afford another notification." At 1%, a dying phone battery confronts the charger that left it waiting by the outlet—and charged the tablet instead—before mourning the connection they used to have.
